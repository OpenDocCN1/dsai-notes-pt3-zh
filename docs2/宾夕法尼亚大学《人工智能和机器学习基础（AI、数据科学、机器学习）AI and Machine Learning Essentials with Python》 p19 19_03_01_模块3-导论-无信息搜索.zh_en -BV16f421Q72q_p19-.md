In artificial intelligence， many of the problems that we'd like to be able to solve involve searching for the best sequence of actions that leads to some goal。

These are called search problems because they often involve searching over many possible combinations of sequences of actions in order to find the optimal solution。

There are many different kinds of problems that can be cast as search problems。

 Everything from navigating a map to solving a puzzle to programming your robot vacuum to clean your house。

 All of these disparate tasks can be thought of as search problems。

 and therefore they can use the same algorithms to find solutions。In this module。

 we'll give a formal definition of search problems and we'll look at two classic algorithms for solving search problems。

 breadth first search and depth first search。These are instances of shortest paths algorithms。

 and they're what are called uninformed search algorithms or blind search algorithms。

 because they don't use any external knowledge about where the goal is located。

In addition to BFS and DFS， we'll look at several variations of these algorithms that have desirable computational complexity properties。
