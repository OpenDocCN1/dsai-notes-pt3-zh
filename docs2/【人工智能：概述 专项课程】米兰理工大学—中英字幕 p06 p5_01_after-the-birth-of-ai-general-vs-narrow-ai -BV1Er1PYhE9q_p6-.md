The initial ambitious goal of AI researchers is what it is usually called general AI。

That is the hypothetical intelligence of a computer program that has the capacity to understand or learn any intellectual task that a human being can。

No successful example how general eye is known today。In contrast to general AI。

 N of AI is a term used to describe artificial intelligence systems that are specified to handle a singular or limited task。

For instance。Examples of NA AI are Siri， Corana， Google Hasistant。
