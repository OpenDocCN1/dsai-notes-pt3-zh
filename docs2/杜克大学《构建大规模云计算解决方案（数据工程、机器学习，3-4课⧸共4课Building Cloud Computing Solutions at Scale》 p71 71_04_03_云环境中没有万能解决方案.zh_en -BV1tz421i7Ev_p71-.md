The CTO of Amazon， Warner Vogels mentioned a few years ago that a one sizeize fit database doesn't fit anybody and what he means by that is that databases have several different characteristics depending on what kind of problem you're solving for let's say a banking operation。

 maybe a transactional database， a traditional relational database would be a great fit because everything is consistent but with a web-based application that's maybe a content management system eventually consistent would be a much better operation for that particular database in other scenarios。

 a graph database that had the ability to look at the different relationships between social networks could be another type of database So really when you're building things in a cloud there's up to let's say a dozen different databases on AWS。

 It's your choice to pick the right database for the right task at hand。

 and just one database by itself may not be the appropriate solution。
