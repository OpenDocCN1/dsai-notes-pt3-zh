 but unconscious human biases that systematically disenfranchise female and nonwh job applicants。

 if this inequality comes from human bias， It seems natural to try and correct it with artificial intelligence。

There are automated hiring managers specifically designed to sort through hundreds of resumes and choose the ideal candidate based on an AI algorithm。

 Unfortunately， there's a problem。 Any AI used to evaluate resumes has the fed example data to create patterns from。

And a database of resumes fed to an AI will teach it， essentially。

 that the ideal worker to hire is when it looks like the workers that are already hired。

And then it should under no circumstances， hire someone who doesn't fit the existing profile。

That is to say AI will replicate existing biases， not eliminate them。

We can see this happening already in various fields。

Automated hiring managers routinely dismiss applicants based on their gender， age， race， disability。

 or other factors that are illegal to discriminate against。😊。

The underlying data informing a variety of technologies tend to be trained on a white male default from medical diagnostic programs to facial recognition on cell phone cameras。

 making them operate suboptily for non male non white users。
