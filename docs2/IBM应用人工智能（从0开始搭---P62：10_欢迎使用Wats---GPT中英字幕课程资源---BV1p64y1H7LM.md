We'll begin your journey by creating your first action。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_8.png)

Put simply an action is a problem or task that your customer wants your assistant to resolve for them。

 When users interact with your assistant， it will guide them into an action based on the questions they ask or statements they make。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_10.png)

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_11.png)

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_12.png)

Just like in a conversation with a human。

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_14.png)

Once an action starts， the conversation is organized into steps。When designing actions。

 you arrange steps in conversational order from top to bottom。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_16.png)

You can rearrange the order of steps by dragging and dropping each one into its desired position。

 Steps are the building blocks of every interaction。

 A well designed conversation will flow seamlessly until the customer's need is met。

 or the conversation is escalated。This is because Watson assistant can understand your customers's requests。

 regardless of the exact wording， the whole time that your assistant and customer are interacting。

 the assistant is gathering information based on your users's input and actively reading and remembering what your users are telling it。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_18.png)

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_19.png)

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_20.png)

As part of building your first action， you'll add multiple example phrases that your users might enter to initiate the action by adding multiple versions of the same request。

 you train your assistant to understand the way humans can use different phrases to express the same needs。

The more example phrases you train your assistant to recognize。

 the easier it will be for your assistant to interpret your user's requests and guide them into the correct action。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_22.png)

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_23.png)

In addition to arranging steps in the correct order to guide users to the actions completion。

 you can enrich the conversation by adding response types。Response types include text buttons， times。

 currency， and more。

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_25.png)

When the user selects an option， that data is stored for use later in the conversation。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_27.png)

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_28.png)

Based on customer responses， your assistant chooses what direction the conversation will take。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_30.png)

It can funnel the user further into the exchange towards a concluding step。

 route the user to a different action， or hand off the conversation to a human agent。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_32.png)

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_33.png)

As you're building your first action， you can preview your assistant's ability to guide users by talking to your assistant in the Preview panel。

The preview panel allows you to continuously test your assistant performance。

This gives you a real time snapshot of how it will perform when interacting with your users。

After you preview your first action and the assistant's ability to guide users to its conclusion。

 it's time to share your assistant with your team。

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_35.png)

Sharing your assistant is the best way to get feedback on your content and improve its ability to guide users through actions to successful completion。



![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_37.png)

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_38.png)

As you test and engage with your assistant， you can edit your steps based on their success rates in completing each action。

![](img/dd9d1a09d6cedb5d5a50fd93a27c0a3f_43.png)

The dashboard contains a task tracker that outlines the build process。

 The tracker is an ideal map for navigating from the initial deployment of your assistant on a test channel to the final publication on a live channel。
