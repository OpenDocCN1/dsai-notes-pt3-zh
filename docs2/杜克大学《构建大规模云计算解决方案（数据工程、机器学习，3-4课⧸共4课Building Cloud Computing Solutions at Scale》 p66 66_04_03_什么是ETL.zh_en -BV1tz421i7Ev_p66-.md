 and it's a common operation in a data engineering pipeline。

 The reason for this is that a lot of times when you're getting the data from a source。

 it isn't exactly the cleanest form and you'll have to extract the data potentially uncompress it or change it from one format to another。

 maybe get rid of corrupted values or missing values。

 transform things together and then load into a new system like， for example。

 the business intelligence database， so in general when you're building data pipelines it's important to think about those three terms。

 extract， transform and load and most data engineering operations involve those things。
