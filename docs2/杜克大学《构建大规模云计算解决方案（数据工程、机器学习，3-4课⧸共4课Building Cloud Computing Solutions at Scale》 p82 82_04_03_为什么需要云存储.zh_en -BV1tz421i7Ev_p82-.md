 they can both purchase a lower cost flower bag for you and you can buy it cheaper because of the size that they're selling it。

 if you purchase things in bulk as a cloud provider， you can then sell it for much less。

 and then if you need to yourself as the customer access large quantities of data or store large quantities of data。

 you actually get access to things at a much lower price。
