There is a tension at the heart of mathematics teaching in the computer age。

 Classical teaching approaches focus around working through lots of examples by hand without much emphasis on building intuition。

However， computers now do nearly all of the calculation work for us。

 and it's not typical for the methods appropriate to hand calculation to be the same as those employed by a computer。

This can mean that despite doing lots of work， students can come away from a classical education。
