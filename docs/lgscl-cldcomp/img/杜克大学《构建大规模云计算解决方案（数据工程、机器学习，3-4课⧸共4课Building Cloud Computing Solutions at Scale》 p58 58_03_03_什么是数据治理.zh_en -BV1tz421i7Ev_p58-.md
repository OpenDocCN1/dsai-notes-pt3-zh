What is data governance Well it's one of the most popular terms in 2020 and beyond and the reason for this is that cloud security is critical to really the health of a company if you're a credit card transaction company and let's say you've got to 100 million users and all of their data is released that could actually shut your whole company down it could be an existential threat to your organization So data governance is really thinking through who should have access to the data。

 what are the worst case scenarios is your data encrypted at rest， is it encrypted in transit。

 these are all key considerations for dealing with data governance and it's something that's very important in fact。

 in many organizations there's a data governance officer。
