What is data engineering This is a great question and let's dig into the details What data engineering is is the ability to build pipelines that transport data or transform data at a periodic basis and it's the software engineering around that data so a data engineer could be doing big data operations with Spar they could be building event- drivenriven infrastructures with AWs Lambda or they could be handling jobs like nightly jobs that collect analytics and create sales forecast for the executive team so a data engineer is focused exclusively on the software engineering best practices around the movement and transport of data。
