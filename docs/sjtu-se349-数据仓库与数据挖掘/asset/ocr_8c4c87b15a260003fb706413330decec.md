# Local Outlier Factor: LOF

+   Reachability distance 可达距离 from o' to o:
+   reachdist_k(o ← o') = max{dist_k(o), dist(o, o')}
+   — k is a user-specified parameter 控制光滑效果

+   Local reachability density 局部可达密度 of o:
+   lrd_k(o) = ||N_k(o)|| / Σ_{o'∈N_k(o)} reachdist_k(o' ← o)

+   LOF (Local outlier factor 局部离群点因子) of an object o is the average of the ratio of local reachability of o and those of o's k-nearest neighbors
+   LOF_k(o) = Σ_{o'∈N_k(o)} lrd_k(o') / lrd_k(o) / ||N_k(o)|| = Σ_{o'∈N_k(o)} lrd_k(o') · Σ_{o'∈N_k(o)} reachdist_k(o' ← o)

+   The lower the local reachability density of o, and the higher the local reachability density of the kNN of o, the higher LOF

+   This captures a local outlier whose local density is relatively low comparing to the local densities of its kNN
