# 模糊集合及其运算

例4:设 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0.1 \\ 0.2 & 0.3 \end{pmatrix}$, $B = \begin{pmatrix} 0.4 & 0 \\ 0.3 & 0.2 \end{pmatrix}$, 则

$A \cup B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0.1 \\ 0.3 & 0.3 \end{pmatrix}$    $A \cap B = \begin{pmatrix} 0.4 & 0 \\ 0.2 & 0.2 \end{pmatrix}$

$A^c = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0.9 \\ 0.8 & 0.7 \end{pmatrix}$    $B^c = \begin{pmatrix} 0.6 & 1 \\ 0.7 & 0.8 \end{pmatrix}$
