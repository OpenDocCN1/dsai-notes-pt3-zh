
## MaPle: Efficient Enumeration of δ-pClusters

- Pei et al., MaPle: Efficiently enumerating all maximal δ-pClusters. ICDM'03
- Framework: Same as pattern-growth in frequent pattern mining (based on the downward closure property)
- For each condition combination J, find the maximal subsets of genes I such that I × J is a δ-pCluster
  - If I × J is not a submatrix of another δ-pCluster, then I × J is a maximal δ-pCluster.
- Algorithm is very similar to mining frequent closed itemsets
- Additional advantages of δ-pClusters:
  - Due to averaging of δ-cluster, it may contain outliers but still within δ-threshold
  - Computing bi-clusters for scaling patterns: taking the logarithm of
    \[
    \frac{d_{xa}/d_{ya}}{d_{xb}/d_{yb}} < \delta
    \]
    will lead to the p-score form

## Dimensionality-Reduction Methods（维规约方法）

Dimensionality reduction: In some situations, it is more effective to construct a new space instead of using some subspaces of the original data.

![](img/6d25e92eb0bd56a22c311279c19b06b4_1334_0.png)

- Ex. To cluster the points in the right figure, any subspace of the original one, X and Y, cannot help, since all the three clusters will be projected into the overlapping areas in X and Y axes.
  - Construct a new dimension as the dashed one, the three clusters become apparent when the points are projected into the new dimension.
- Dimensionality reduction methods
  - Feature selection and extraction: But may not focus on clustering structure finding
  - Spectral clustering: Combining feature extraction and clustering (i.e., use the *spectrum* of the similarity matrix of the data to perform dimensionality reduction for clustering in fewer dimensions)
    - Normalized Cuts (Shi and Malik, CVPR’97 or PAMI 2000)
    - The Ng-Jordan-Weiss algorithm (NIPS’01)

### Spectral Clustering: Illustration and Comments

- Data → Affinity matrix → Computing the leading k eigenvectors of A → Clustering in the new space → Projecting back to cluster the original data

\[
W_{ij}; \quad A = f(W); \quad Av = \lambda v; \quad V = [v_1, v_2, v_3]; \quad U = [u_1, u_2, u_3]
\]

- Spectral clustering: Effective in tasks like image processing
- Scalability challenge: Computing eigenvectors on a large matrix is costly
- Can be combined with other clustering methods, such as bi-clustering

## Clustering Graphs and Network Data

- Applications
  - Bipartite graphs（偶图）, e.g., customers and products, authors and conferences
  - Web search engines, e.g., click-through graphs（查询） and Web graphs（网页），通过 Web 图可以检测权威网页和垃圾网页
  - Social networks, friendship/coauthor graphs

前面所有方法都是给出对象及其一组属性，而图和网络数据的独有特征是只给出了对象（或顶点）和它们之间的联系（边），没有定义属性或维。为了进行聚类，需要解决两个难点：

- Similarity measures
  - 欧式距离等传统距离度量失灵，需要开发新的测度来量化相似性
  - Geodesic distances
  - Distance based on random walk (SimRank)
- Graph clustering methods
  - 携带了更复杂的拓扑结构，数据集都很大
  - Minimum cuts: FastModularity (Clauset, Moore, 2004)
  - Density-based clustering: SCAN (Xu et al., 2007)

### Similarity Measure (I): Geodesic Distance（测地距）

![](img/6d25e92eb0bd56a22c311279c19b06b4_1339_0.png)

- Geodesic distance (A, B): length (i.e., # of edges) of the shortest path between A and B (if not connected, defined as infinite（无穷大）)
- Eccentricity（离心率） of v, eccen(v): The largest geodesic distance between v and any other vertex \(u \in V - \{v\}\)。捕获了 v 与图中最远顶点的远近程度
- E.g., eccen(a) = eccen(b) = 2; eccen(c) = eccen(d) = eccen(e) = 3
- Radius of graph G（图G的半径）: The minimum eccentricity of all vertices, i.e., the distance between the “most central point” and the “farthest border”，捕获了图中“最靠近中心的点”与“最远边界”之间的距离
- \(r = \min_{v\in V} \mathrm{eccen}(v)\)
- E.g., radius(g) = 2
- Diameter of graph G: The maximum eccentricity of all vertices, i.e., the largest distance between any pair of vertices in G
- \(d = \max_{v\in V} \mathrm{eccen}(v)\)
- E.g., diameter(g) = 3
- A peripheral vertex（外围顶点） is a vertex that achieves the diameter.
- E.g., Vertices c, d, and e are peripheral vertices.
