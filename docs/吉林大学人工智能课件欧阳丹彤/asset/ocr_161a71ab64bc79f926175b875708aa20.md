A1=∃x (P(x) ∧ ∀y(~D(y) ∨ L(x, y)))
=∃x ∀y (P(x) ∧ (~D(y) ∨ L(x, y)))
----> ∀y (P(a) ∧ (~D(y) ∨ L(a, y)))
A2=∀x(P(x) → ∀y(~Q(y) ∨ ~L(x, y)))
=∀x(~P(x) ∨ ∀y(~Q(y) ∨ ~L(x, y)))
=∀x∀y (~P(x) ∨ ~Q(y) ∨ ~L(x, y))
~B=~(∀x(D(x) → ~Q(x)))
=∃x (D(x) ∧ Q(x))
----> D(b) ∧ Q(b)
因此，公式A1∧A2∧~B的子句集为
S= {P(a), ~D(y)∨L(a,y), ~P(x)∨~Q(y)∨~L(x, y), D(b), Q(b)}
