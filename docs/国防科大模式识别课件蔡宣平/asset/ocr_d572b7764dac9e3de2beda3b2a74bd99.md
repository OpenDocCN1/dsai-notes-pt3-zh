两类均作为正态分布，并假设 $P(ω_1)=P(ω_2)$ ，故判决式为

```
$d_i(\vec{x}) = \vec{x}' C^{-1} \vec{m}_i - \frac{1}{2} \vec{m}_i' C^{-1} \vec{m}_i$
```

$\vec{m}_1 = \frac{1}{4}(3,1,1)' \quad \vec{m}_2 = \frac{1}{4}(1,3,3)' \quad C^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 & -4 & -4 \\ -4 & 8 & 4 \\ -4 & 4 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$

$d_1(\vec{x}) = 4x_1 - \frac{3}{2} \quad d_2(\vec{x}) = -4x_1 + 8x_2 + 8x_3 - \frac{11}{2}$

$d_1(\vec{x}) - d_2(\vec{x}) = 8x_1 - 8x_2 - 8x_3 + 4 = 0$

$2x_1 - 2x_2 - 2x_3 + 1 = 0$
