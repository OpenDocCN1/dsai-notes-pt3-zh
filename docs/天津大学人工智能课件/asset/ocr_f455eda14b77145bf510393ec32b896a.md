# 条件： \( W = (\forall x)[ (\exists y)(U(x, y) \land I(y)) \to (\exists u)(F(u) \land E(x, u)) ] \)
变换为子句集。

+   (1) 消去蕴含 → 办法： \( P(x) \to Q(x) \iff \sim P(x) \lor Q(x) \)
+   
+   \( W = (\forall x)\{ \sim [ (\exists y)(U(x, y) \land I(y)) ] \lor (\exists u)(F(u) \land E(x, u)) \} \)

+   (2) 把～的辖域减少到最多只作用于一个谓词
+   
+   利用 \( \sim(P(x) \land Q(x)) \iff \sim P(x) \lor Q(x) \)； \( \sim(\exists x)P(x) \iff (\forall x)[\sim P(x)] \)
+   
+   \( W = (\forall x) \{ (\forall y)[\sim(U(x, y) \land I(y))] \lor (\exists u) (F(u) \land E(x, u)) \} \)
+   
+   \( = (\forall x) \{ (\forall y) [\sim U(x, y) \lor \sim I(y)] \lor (\exists u) (F(u) \land E(x, u)) \} \)

+   (3) 消存在量词,形成S范式
+   
+   \( W' = (\forall x) \{ (\forall y) \{ [\sim U(x, y) \lor \sim I(y)] \lor [F(f(x)) \land E(x, f(x))] \} \)
