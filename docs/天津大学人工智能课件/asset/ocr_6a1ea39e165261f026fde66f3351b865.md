# 结论：\( G=\neg(\exists u)F(u)\rightarrow(\forall x)(\forall y)(I(y)\rightarrow\neg U(x, y))\)

否定结论\( G \)，并化为S范式

\(\neg G = \neg[\neg(\exists u)F(u)\rightarrow(\forall x)(\forall y)(I(y)\rightarrow\neg U(x, y))]\)

\( =\neg[(\exists u)F(u)\lor(\forall x)(\forall y)(\neg I(y)\lor\neg U(x, y))]\)

\( =\neg(\exists u)F(u)\land\neg[(\forall x)(\forall y)(\neg I(y)\lor\neg U(x, y))]\)

\( =(\forall x)(\forall y)[\neg(\exists u)F(u)\land I(y)\land U(x, y)]\)

引入常量c，消去\(\neg G\)中的存在量词，得

\((\forall x)(\forall y)[\neg F(c)\land I(y)\land U(x, y)]\)

隐去全称量词，得到\( G \)对应子句集的三个子句：

\(\neg F(c) \quad I(y) \quad U(x, y)\)
