# # 例2 更新法求结论的可信度

已知：规则可信度为

R1: A→X CF(X,A)=0.8； R2: B→X CF(X,B)=0.6

R3: C→X CF(X,C)=0.4； R4: X∧D→Y CF(Y, X∧D)=0.3

CF(A)=CF(B)=CF(C)=CF(D)=0.5。

X、Y的初始可信度CF₀(X)=0.1， CF₀(Y)=0.2。

求：(1)结论X的可信度CF(X); (0.698) (2)结论Y的可信度CF(Y); (0.32)

解：(1) CF(X|A)=CF₀(X)+CF (A) × CF (X,A) –CF₀(X) × CF (A) ×CF(X,A)

=0.1+0.5 × 0.8–0.1 × 0.5 × 0.8 = 0.46

CF(X|A,B)=CF(X|A)+CF (B) × CF (X,B) –CF (X|A) × CF (B) ×CF(X,B)

=0.46+0.5 × 0.6–0.46 × 0.5 × 0.6 = 0.622

CF(X|A,B,C)= 0.698

(2) CF(Y| X∧D)=CF₀(Y)+CF (X∧D) × CF (Y, X∧D)

–CF₀(Y) × CF (X∧D) ×CF(Y, X∧D)=0.32
