# 代表性的制造业信息系统——MRP

![](bbox=[0.1, 0.25, 0.25, 0.38])

MRP (Materials Requirements Planning) is basically an Ordering Tool.

![](bbox=[0.1, 0.42, 0.32, 0.68])

+   - Originated in USA in early 60s by Dr. Joseph Orlicky in IBM plant.
+   - Planning of materials acquisition & production.
+   - MPS - what are we going to make?
+     BOM - what does it take to make?
+     Inventory Records
+     - what do we have?
+     - what do we need to get?
