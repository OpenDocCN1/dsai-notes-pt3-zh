# Why Is Data Preprocessing Important?

+   - No quality data, no quality mining results!
+     - How about mining those data directly?
+       - although most mining routines have some ability of dealing with incomplete or noisy data, they are not always robust
+       - most mining routines cannot deal with inconsistency
+       - even without incompleteness, noise, and inconsistency, the size of the data may be too large to mine

Data preprocessing usually occupies about 70% workload in a data mining task
