## 5.3 其它频繁模式与关联规则挖掘方法

### Problem with the frequent pattern mining algorithm

- Given a database :
  - DB={(a₁,a₂,...,a₁₀₀),(a₁,a₂,...,a₅₀)}
  - The min_support = 1, and the min_conf= 50%
- The traditional association mining will generate 2¹⁰⁰-1≈10³⁰ frequent itemsets:
  - (a₁), (a₂),..., (a₁₀₀),
  - (a₁,a₂), (a₂,a₃),..., (a₉₉,a₁₀₀),
  - ...,
  - (a₁,a₂,...,a₁₀₀)
  - and tremendous number of association rules.

Too many frequent patterns if the support threshold is low!

- Mining closed frequent itemsets and max-patterns
  - CLOSET (DMKD’00)
- Mining sequential patterns
  - GSP (EDBT’96), PrefixSpan (ICDE’01)
- Constraint-based mining of frequent patterns
  - Convertible constraints (KDD’00, ICDE’01)
- Computing iceberg data cubes with complex measures
  - H-tree and H-cubing algorithm (SIGMOD’01)

### Closed Patterns and Max-Patterns

**Closed pattern**

- An itemset X is closed if X is frequent and there exists no super-pattern Y ⊃ X, with the same support as X
- proposed by Pasquier, et al. (ICDT’99)
- Example
  - ‘acdf’, ‘cef’, ‘ae’,
  - ‘cf’, ‘a’, and ‘e’
  - are frequent closed itemsets

A lossless compression of freq. patterns mining!

Min_sup=2

| TID | Items |
| --- | ----- |
| 10 | a, c, d, e, f |
| 20 | a, b, e |
| 30 | c, e, f |
| 40 | a, c, d, f |
| 50 | c, e, f |

- Max-patterns: borders of frequent patterns
  - A subset of max-pattern is frequent
  - A superset of max-pattern is infrequent
- 如果频繁项集F 的所有超集都是非频繁项集，那么称F为最大频繁项集，所有F的集合称为最大频繁项集集合 (maximum frequent sets).
- 显然，任何频繁项集都是最大频繁项集的子集。
- 发现所有频繁项集的问题可以转化为发现所有最大频繁项集的问题.
- BCDE, ACD are max-patterns
- BCD is not a max-pattern

### 支持度-置信度框架的局限

- 支持度的缺陷
  - 很多潜在的有意义的模式由于包含支持度小的项而被删除
- 置信度问题
  - 例如：
    - 产生关联规则：{茶} -> {咖啡}
    - 支持度：15%，置信度75%

| | 咖啡 | 非咖啡 | 合计 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 茶 | 150 | 50 | 200 |
| 非茶 | 650 | 150 | 800 |
| 合计 | 800 | 200 | 1000 |

但是，所有人中，不管他是否喝茶，喝咖啡的人的比例为80%，而喝咖啡的饮者却只占75%。

这意味着，一个人如果喝茶，则他喝咖啡的可能性由80%降到75%。

因此，尽管规则{茶} -> {咖啡}有很高的置信度，但是它却是一个误导！

> Strong association rules may not be interesting

- 置信度
  - 缺陷：忽略了规则后件中出现的项集的支持度
  - 如例中，饮茶者和喝咖啡的人之间存在着一种逆关系
- 需要其它客观度量
  - 提升度
  - χ²统计
  - 全置信度
  - 余弦度量
  - ……

### 其它兴趣度度量

- 提升度(lift): A和B两个项集的出现之间的提升度
- Measure of dependent/correlated events
- the lift of the association rule A⇒B is defined as

  lift(A ⇒ B) = P(A ∩ B) / [P(A)P(B)] = P(B|A) / P(B)

- lift = 1 A and B are independent
- lift > 1 the occurrence of A encourages the occurrence of B(正相关)
- lift < 1 the occurrence of A discourages the occurrence of B (负相关)
- the bigger the value of lift, the stronger the correlation between A and B

| | game | Not game | Sum (row) |
|---|---|---|---|
| video | 4000 | 3500 | 7500 |
| Not video | 2000 | 500 | 2500 |
| Sum(col.) | 6000 | 4000 | 10000 |
