# To Normalize

+   - (Dictionary) To bring or restore to a normal condition

+   - Procedure:
+     - Step 1: Compute Z = sum over all entries
+     - Step 2: Divide every entry by Z

+   - Example 1

| W | P |
|---|---|
| sun | 0.2 |
| rain | 0.3 |

Normalize
Z = 0.5

| W | P |
|---|---|
| sun | 0.4 |
| rain | 0.6 |

+   - Example 2

| T | W | P |
|---|---|---|
| hot | sun | 20 |
| hot | rain | 5 |
| cold | sun | 10 |
| cold | rain | 15 |

Normalize
Z = 50

| T | W | P |
|---|---|---|
| hot | sun | 0.4 |
| hot | rain | 0.1 |
| cold | sun | 0.2 |
| cold | rain | 0.3 |

All entries sum to ONE
