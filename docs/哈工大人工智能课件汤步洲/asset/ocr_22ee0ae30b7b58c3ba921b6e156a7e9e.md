# Normalization Trick

P(T, W)

| T | W | P |
|---|---|---|
| hot | sun | 0.4 |
| hot | rain | 0.1 |
| cold | sun | 0.2 |
| cold | rain | 0.3 |

SELECT the joint probabilities matching the evidence

P(c, W)

| T | W | P |
|---|---|---|
| cold | sun | 0.2 |
| cold | rain | 0.3 |

NORMALIZE the selection (make it sum to one)

P(W|T = c)

| W | P |
|---|---|
| sun | 0.4 |
| rain | 0.6 |

Why does this work? Sum of selection is P(evidence) (P(T=c), here)

```
P(x1|x2) = P(x1, x2) / P(x2) = P(x1, x2) / Σx1 P(x1, x2)
```
