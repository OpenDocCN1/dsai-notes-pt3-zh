# Conditional Distributions

+   - Conditional distributions are probability distributions over some variables given fixed values of others

Conditional Distributions

P(W|T)

P(W|T = hot)

| W | P |
|---|---|
| sun | 0.8 |
| rain | 0.2 |

P(W|T = cold)

| W | P |
|---|---|
| sun | 0.4 |
| rain | 0.6 |

Joint Distribution

P(T, W)

| T | W | P |
|---|---|---|
| hot | sun | 0.4 |
| hot | rain | 0.1 |
| cold | sun | 0.2 |
| cold | rain | 0.3 |
