Human evaluation is a critical component to any evaluation of X AI systems。

 This will likely start with you evaluating the explanations for quality。

As you build X AI systems that you want humans to effectively interact with。

 having other people evaluate your system is essential。

Through user studies and cognitive experiments， you can evaluate your XAI system。



![](img/d1590756745c0c3c792420810a7229f4_2.png)

First， human evaluation consists of user studies which involve end users or domain experts evaluating the quality。

 understandability and usefulness of the explanations through surveys。

 interviews or controlled experiments。

![](img/d1590756745c0c3c792420810a7229f4_4.png)

Common evaluation metrics for user studies include likert scales。

 which are a technique used to scale responses that involve a sum of responses。

 You have probably seen them in a number of forms。 One common one is the agree， strongly agree。

 disagree， strongly disagree or neither agree nor disagree question。

Other evaluation metrics include rating scores， task performance measures such as accuracy or time and qualitative feedback。

Another human evaluation approach is cognitive experiments in which the cognitive impact of explanations is assessed。

 such as how they influence human decision making， trust or mental models。



![](img/d1590756745c0c3c792420810a7229f4_6.png)

Typical evaluation metrics for cognitive experiments include eye tracking data， response times。

 decision quality， and trust measures。
