式(3.2-11)后三区部都是 $\varepsilon$ 的高阶小量，故有

$$\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}J=\int_{\sigma}^{\sigma+d}\frac{\partial S[X^*(t_f)]}{\partial X^T(t_f)}\Phi(t_f,\tau)\{f[X^*(\tau),U^*(\tau)+\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}U(\tau)]-f[X^*(\tau),U^*(\tau)]\}\,d\tau+o(\varepsilon)$$

令

$$\bar{\lambda}^{T}(\tau)=\frac{\partial S[X^*(t_f)]}{\partial X^T(t_f)}\Phi(t_f,\tau)$$

则 $\lambda(t)$ 必须满足状态方程

$$\dot{X}(t)=f(X,U),\quad X(t_0)=X_0,\ t\in[t_0,t_f]$$

的共轭方程

$$\dot{\bar{\lambda}}(t)=-\frac{\partial f^T[X^*(t),U^*(t)]}{\partial X}\bar{\lambda}(t)$$

$$=-\frac{\partial H[X^*(t),\bar{\lambda},U^*(t)]}{\partial X}$$

其中

$$\begin{cases}H[X(t),\lambda(t),U(t)]=\lambda^T f(X,U)\\\bar{\lambda}(t_f)=\dfrac{\partial S[X^*(t_f)]}{\partial X(t_f)}\end{cases}$$

可得

$$\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}J=\int_{\sigma}^{\sigma+d}\{H[X^*(\tau),\lambda(\tau),U^*(\tau)+\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}U(\tau)]-H[X^*(\tau),\lambda(\tau),U^*(\tau)]\}\,d\tau$$

$$\qquad +o(\varepsilon)$$

# 4.极值条件的推证

因为 $U^*(t)$ 为最优控制，即使 $J$ 为最小值，故有

$$\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}J=\int_{\sigma}^{\sigma+d}\{H[X^*(\tau),\lambda(\tau),U^*(\tau)+\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}U(\tau)]-H[X^*(\tau),\lambda(\tau),U^*(\tau)]\}\,d\tau+o(\varepsilon)$$

$$\qquad\geq 0$$

根据中值定理及 $H$ 的连续性，有

$$\int_{\sigma}^{\sigma+d}\{H[X^*(\tau),\lambda(\tau),U^*(\tau)+\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}U(\tau)]-H[X^*(\tau),\lambda(\tau),U^*(\tau)]\}\,d\tau$$

$$=\varepsilon\,\{H[X^*(t_1),\lambda(t_1),U^*(t_1)+\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}U(t_1)]-H[X^*(t_1),\lambda(t_1),U^*(t_1)]\}d$$

其中 $t_1=\sigma+\theta d，\ 0\leq\theta\leq1$。

由(3.2-17)和(3.2-18)得

$$\{H[X^*(t_1),\lambda(t_1),U^*(t_1)+\Delta_{\sigma\varepsilon}U(t_1)]-H[X^*(t_1),\lambda(t_1),U^*(t_1)]\}+\frac{o(\varepsilon)}{\varepsilon}\geq0$$

当 $\varepsilon\to0$ 时，有

$$H[X^*(\sigma),\lambda(\sigma),\bar{U}(\sigma)]-H[X^*(\sigma),\lambda(\sigma),U^*(\sigma)]\geq0$$
