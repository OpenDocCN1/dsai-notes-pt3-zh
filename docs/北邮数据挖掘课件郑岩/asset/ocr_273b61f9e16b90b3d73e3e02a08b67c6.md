# Data Structure

+   - Data structured into three parts
+   - Relations have
+   - Attributes which have

Relation about cups of coffee

coffee has **attributes** of **size**, **sugar**, **temperature** etc

**size** has **instances** of **small**, **medium**, **large**
**sugar** has **instances** of **yes** or **no**
**temperature** has **instances** of **39.8**, **54.7** or **41.0** Celsius
