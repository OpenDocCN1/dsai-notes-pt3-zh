## ConvNet Architecture

### Convolution Layer

32x32x3 image
5x5x3 filter _w_

1 number:
the result of taking a dot product between the
filter and a small 5x5x3 chunk of the image
(i.e. 5*5*3 = 75-dimensional dot product + bias)
_w<sup>T</sup>x + b_
